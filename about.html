OUR PROFILE

Connecting requirements with global supply.

Established in 2003, K.K. International is a Pakistan-based sole proprietorship engaged in import, export, wholesale trading and international sourcing.

Our imports include textile yarn, industrial machinery, agricultural products, pesticides, fungicides and other merchandise. Our exports include textile products, rice, fresh fruits, safety matches and other products. We have exported to Malaysia, Turkey, Turkmenistan, Afghanistan and Indonesia, and import from Malaysia and China.

Our operations are supported by our Pakistan office and our corporate office in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. PACIFIC MEGA MARKETING SDN. BHD. is one of the largest suppliers in Malaysia and Indonesia of traditional samping, songket textiles, Baju Melayu and related products. The company also supports international marketing, buyer development and supplier coordination. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know about us.

Find quick answers about K.K. International, our trading activities and how to start a business enquiry.

When was K.K. International established?

K.K. International was established in 2003.

Where is K.K. International based?

K.K. International is a Pakistan-based sole proprietorship with its office in Multan, Pakistan.

What products does K.K. International trade?

Our import interests include textile yarn, industrial machinery, agricultural products, pesticides, fungicides and other merchandise. Our export interests include textile products, rice, fresh fruits, safety matches and other products. Product specifications and availability are confirmed for each enquiry.

Which countries do you trade with?

We have exported to Malaysia, Turkey, Turkmenistan, Afghanistan and Indonesia. Our imports include products sourced from Malaysia and China. Trading opportunities are considered according to product requirements and destination.

What is the relationship with PACIFIC MEGA MARKETING SDN. BHD.?

PACIFIC MEGA MARKETING SDN. BHD. is a separate Malaysian company based in Kuala Lumpur. It is associated with our international business activities and has expertise in traditional samping, songket textiles, Baju Melayu and related products.

How can I enquire about a product or become a business partner?

Use our Contact page to reach the Pakistan or Malaysia office. Share the product name, specifications, required quantity and destination so we can understand your enquiry.
