OUR PROFILE

Connecting requirements with global supply.

Established in 2003, K.K. International is a Pakistan-based sole proprietorship engaged in import, export, wholesale trading and international sourcing.

Our imports include textile yarn, industrial machinery, agricultural products, pesticides, fungicides and other merchandise. Our exports include textile products, rice, fresh fruits, safety matches and other products. We have exported to Malaysia, Turkey, Turkmenistan, Afghanistan and Indonesia, and import from Malaysia and China.

Our operations are supported by our Pakistan office and our corporate office in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. PACIFIC MEGA MARKETING SDN. BHD. is one of the largest suppliers in Malaysia and Indonesia of traditional samping, songket textiles, Baju Melayu and related products. The company also supports international marketing, buyer development and supplier coordination.
