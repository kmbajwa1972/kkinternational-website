OUR PROFILE

Connecting requirements with global supply.

Established in 2003, K.K. International is a Pakistan-based sole proprietorship engaged in import, export, wholesale trading and international sourcing.

Our import interests include textile yarn, industrial machinery, agricultural products, pesticides, fungicides and related merchandise. Our exports include rice, fresh fruits, textile products and safety matches.

We work with manufacturers, overseas buyers, buying houses and trading agents. Our business includes both direct trading and commission-based import and export transactions.

Pacific Mega Marketing SDN. BHD. is our marketing and trading partner in Malaysia, supporting international business development, buyer coordination and trading opportunities.
