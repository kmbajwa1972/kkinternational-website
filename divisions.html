What we do.

K.K. International operates across a range of import, export and wholesale trading sectors, connecting product requirements with international suppliers, buyers and trading partners.

01

Agriculture & Crop Protection ↗

Import and trading enquiries covering agricultural products, fertilizers, pesticides and fungicides. Product specifications, registration requirements and availability are considered for each enquiry.

02

Textile Trading ↗

Import interests include textile yarn, while export activities include textile fabrics and finished textile products for international buyers and trading partners.

03

Food & Commodities ↗

Export trading interests include rice, fresh fruits and safety matches, with product specifications, quantities and destination requirements considered for each transaction.

04

Industrial Machinery & Global Sourcing ↗

International sourcing and import enquiries for industrial machinery and related products, alongside supplier coordination, buyer requirements and wholesale trading opportunities.
