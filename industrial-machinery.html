Industrial Machinery

Sourcing coordination for machinery, processing equipment and related products.

K.K. International handles import enquiries for industrial machinery and processing equipment. We work from the technical and commercial requirements supplied by the buyer to help clarify the equipment being sought.

01 / TECHNICAL ENQUIRY

Technical Enquiry

A machinery enquiry should include the machine name and intended use, capacity, power requirements, operating conditions, new or used status, preferred origin and any available technical catalogue or photographs.

02 / SOURCING & SHIPMENT DETAILS

Sourcing & Shipment Details

Supplier information, equipment specifications, packing, inspection expectations, documentation and shipment arrangements are considered individually. Final suitability and availability are subject to supplier confirmation.
