Textile & Industrial Trading

Connecting textile materials and industrial requirements with sourcing opportunities.

Textile Materials

Our textile interests include yarn imports and the export of fabrics and finished textile products. Enquiries can include yarn count, composition, construction, colour, finish, quantity and destination.

02 / INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT

Industrial Equipment
