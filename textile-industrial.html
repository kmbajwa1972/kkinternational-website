K.K. INTERNATIONAL · BUSINESS PORTFOLIO

Textile Trading

Songket, fabrics and finished textile products for international trade.

K.K. International supports textile trade enquiries covering yarn, fabrics and finished textile products. We work with buyers and suppliers to understand product specifications, coordinate sourcing requirements and facilitate import and export opportunities in line with each customer’s needs.

01 / SONGKET & TEXTILE EXPORTS

Songket & Textile Exports

K.K. International supports export enquiries for traditional Songket fabrics and other textile products, connecting international buyers with suitable products and suppliers. We coordinate fabric designs, colours, quality requirements, order quantities, packing and shipment arrangements according to each buyer’s needs.

02 / FABRICS & FINISHED TEXTILES

Fabrics & Finished Textiles

Our textile trading activities also cover fabric and finished textile product enquiries. Buyers can share details such as fabric construction, fibre content, weight, width, colour, print or finish, intended use, order quantity and delivery destination.
