PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy.

How K.K. International handles information when you visit our website or use KK Trade Research.

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

K.K. International is a Pakistan-based sole proprietorship engaged in import, export, wholesale trading and sourcing. For privacy questions, contact us at info@kkinternational.com.pk.

2. Scope

This policy applies to https://kkinternational.com.pk and the public trade-information assistant known as KK Trade Research (the “Service”). It does not cover third-party websites, platforms or services that have their own privacy policies.

3. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us or submit a business enquiry through our website, we may receive the information you provide, such as your name, company, business email, telephone number, country, product interests, quantities and message. We use this information to respond to enquiries and conduct business communications.

KK Trade Research provides general company information, trade-planning assistance, calculations, draft requests and research briefs. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, confidential customer or supplier records, or other sensitive personal or commercially confidential information. The Service is not a private customer relationship management system.

4. How the assistant processes requests

When you use KK Trade Research, the text and parameters you submit are transmitted to the assistant service so it can produce the requested response. The Service may process the request through its hosting infrastructure. We do not use the public assistant to access K.K. International’s private Trade Desk records, mailboxes, customer files or staff accounts.

The Service is not designed to create a user account or maintain a personal profile. We do not intentionally use assistant requests to build advertising profiles or sell personal information. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited technical information, including connection data and server logs, for delivery, security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention, in accordance with their own terms and policies.

5. Purposes and legal basis

We use information to answer enquiries, provide requested trade assistance, operate and secure our website and Service, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is based on your request or consent, our legitimate business interests in operating and securing the service, or compliance with legal obligations.

6. Sharing and service providers

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website or assistant infrastructure, and where necessary with professional advisers, regulators or public authorities. We do not sell personal information. We do not authorise service providers to use information for their own unrelated marketing.

7. Retention and security

We retain business enquiries for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records and meet legal requirements. Technical logs may be retained by infrastructure providers for operational and security purposes under their retention practices. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. International processing

Our website, assistant and service providers may operate in more than one country. Information may therefore be processed outside your country, subject to applicable legal requirements and the safeguards available from the relevant providers.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email info@kkinternational.com.pk. We may need to verify your request and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

10. Children

The website and Service are intended for business and general informational use, not for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, Service or applicable requirements change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact K.K. International at info@kkinternational.com.pk or visit our Contact page.
