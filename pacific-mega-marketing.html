ABOUT PACIFIC MEGA MARKETING

Malaysian roots.
International connections.

PACIFIC MEGA MARKETING SDN. BHD. is our Malaysian company based in Kuala Lumpur.

The company is one of the largest suppliers in Malaysia and Indonesia of traditional samping, songket textiles, Baju Melayu and related products. Its product expertise connects traditional textile offerings with buyers and business opportunities across regional and international markets.

Its activities also support international marketing, buyer development and supplier coordination, working alongside the wider business network of K.K. International.
