INTERNATIONAL TRADING

Direct trading. Commission-based services.

K.K. International is a Pakistan-based trading house established in 2003. We work with buyers, manufacturers and suppliers to source products, agree commercial terms and coordinate international transactions.

We trade on our own account as buyers and sellers, and we also undertake import and export transactions on commission for clients. Our export products include rice, safety matches, fresh fruit and textile products. Our imports include textile yarn and industrial machinery from China.

OUR SERVICES

What we do for every order.

01

International Supplier Sourcing

We identify potential manufacturers and suppliers according to product specifications, quality requirements, quantity and target budget.

02

Quotations & Samples

We coordinate supplier quotations, product details and samples so buyers can evaluate available options before making a purchasing decision.

03

Trade Documentation

We prepare or coordinate commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, shipping documents and other paperwork required for each transaction.

04

Shipping & Customs Coordination

We work with freight forwarders, shipping companies and customs-clearing agents to coordinate shipment arrangements and documentation.

05

Commercial & Payment Coordination

We help buyers and sellers agree practical commercial terms, payment arrangements and transaction requirements for each deal.

WORK WITH US

Two ways to work with K.K. International.

01

Direct Trading — Own Account

We purchase products directly from manufacturers or suppliers and resell them to buyers. We also purchase products directly from sellers. Prices, quantities and commercial terms are agreed for each transaction.

02

Commission-Based Import & Export

We source products and coordinate international buying or selling transactions for clients in return for an agreed commission. Responsibilities and commercial terms are confirmed before the transaction begins.
